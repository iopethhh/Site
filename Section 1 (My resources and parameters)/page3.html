Distribution of free-pick heroes cards

On this page you can distribute free-pick cards between heroes.

For your convenience, there are three buttons: MAX points, MAX stars and Reset


The MAX points will automatically distribute the self-selection cards so that to "squeeze" the maximum out of your heroes, and to score the most possible points on the Alliance Duel/Ultimate Power. Simply put - uses the maximum possible number of cards.

If you have a lot of free-pick heroes cards, this button will not spend them all. It will distribute the cards where necessary, for "maximum squeeze", and you can distribute the remaining cards yourself at your discretion. There will be no difference in points.


The MAX Stars button will automatically distribute the free-pick heroes cards to upgrade as many stars as possible for your heroes. The button will stop when no one can raise a new star with the remaining number of free-pick heroes cards.


The Reset button will perform a complete reset of the self-selection card distribution.


You can "combine" these buttons by pressing first one, then the second, in any order. Or you can use only one of them, and distribute the remaining free-pick cards yourself, at your discretion.
